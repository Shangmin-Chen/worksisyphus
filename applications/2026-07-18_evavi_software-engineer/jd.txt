JD Company Description

Evavi, Inc. is reinventing supply chain management software to help organizations unlock new value across their supply chains. Our core innovation, Supply Chain Synchronization, is an AI-native alternative to traditional legacy applications. By combining modern supply chain planning techniques, unified data ontologies, and agentic workflows, Evavi helps companies build more responsive, efficient, and integrated supply chain operations.

We are a small, fast-moving team building cutting-edge enterprise software for complex, real-world operational problems.

Role Description

Evavi is looking for a full-time, in-person Software Engineer based in Boston. This role is ideal for someone with 0–2 years of experience who wants to grow quickly while working on meaningful full-stack software used to solve supply chain planning and operations challenges.

You will help design, build, test, and maintain features across Evavi’s AI-native supply chain platform, including user-facing web applications, backend services, APIs, data workflows, and internal tools. You will work closely with engineering, product, and data team members to turn business requirements into working software.

This is a hands-on role on a small team, so the right candidate should be comfortable learning quickly, adapting to changing priorities, and contributing across different parts of the product.

Responsibilities

    Build and maintain full-stack web application features.
    Contribute to frontend interfaces, backend services, APIs, and internal tools.
    Write clean, maintainable, and well-tested code.
    Work with Python, TypeScript, and modern web development frameworks.
    Participate in code reviews, debugging, and technical design discussions.
    Help improve system reliability, performance, security, usability, and developer tooling.
    Adapt to dynamic requirements in a fast-paced startup environment.

Qualifications

    0–2 years of experience in software development or web application development.
    Solid foundation in computer science fundamentals, including data structures, algorithms, and systems concepts.
    Experience with Python and TypeScript is desired.
    Familiarity with relational databases and pub-sub technologies such as Kafka is desired.
    Understanding of object-oriented programming and software design principles.
    Ability to work in person in Boston.
    Strong communication skills and comfort working closely with a small team.
    Ability to respond to changing requirements and contribute across the stack.
    Interest in AI-drive systems, supply chain planning, operations, or enterprise SaaS is a bonus.
