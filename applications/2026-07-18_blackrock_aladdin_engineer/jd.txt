Aladdin is BlackRock's industry-leading investment and risk management platform, used by BlackRock and many of the world's largest financial institutions to manage portfolios, analyze risk, and support investment decision-making. As an engineer, you'll help build the technology that powers these capabilities, working on challenges across cloud computing, distributed systems, AI, analytics, and user-facing applications.

It's a strong entry point if you're looking to build a foundation in engineering, with:

    Exposure to one of the world's leading fintech platforms and collaboration with experienced engineers, product managers, and business leaders
    Opportunities to work on products used across global markets and asset classes
    Early ownership of meaningful, high-impact work that directly influences how investors manage risk and make decisions
